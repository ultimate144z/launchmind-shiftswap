ShiftSwap: Empowering Hourly Workers to Exchange Shifts Fairly

Our Value Proposition

ShiftSwap empowers hourly workers to efficiently exchange shifts with real-time approvals, ensuring fair practices and adherence to company policies, thus enhancing workforce flexibility and job satisfaction.

Main Features

  • User Registration: Provide a simple registration process for users to create an account using email or social media logins.
  • Shift Posting: Allow workers to post available shifts they wish to exchange, including necessary details such as date, time, and location.
  • Shift Browsing: Enable users to browse available shifts posted by other workers in a user-friendly interface, with filters for date, role, and work location.
  • Approval Workflow: Implement a straightforward approval mechanism for managers to review and approve shift exchanges, ensuring compliance with company policies.
  • User Notifications: Send notifications to users about shift requests, approvals, and reminders, keeping everyone updated on their schedule.
  • Compliance Guidelines: Outline and display relevant labor laws and company-specific policies to ensure users understand their rights and responsibilities in shift exchanges.
  • Rating and Feedback System: Allow users to rate their shift swap experiences and provide feedback to enhance platform trust and user experience.

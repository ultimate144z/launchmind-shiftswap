Welcome to ShiftSwap

ShiftSwap provides a fast, fair, and compliant platform for hourly workers to easily exchange shifts, increasing flexibility and job satisfaction while ensuring managerial oversight and adherence to company policies.

Features

Shift Exchange Marketplace

A user-friendly interface for hourly workers to post available shifts and request shifts from colleagues, allowing for streamlined exchange.

Manager Approval Workflow

A built-in approval system for managers to review and approve shift exchanges, ensuring compliance with company policies.

Policy Compliance Check

Automated checks against organizational policies to ensure all shift swaps adhere to labor laws and company guidelines.

Instant Notifications

Real-time alerts for employees when shifts are requested or approved, improving communication and response time.

Feedback and Reporting System

An integrated mechanism for users to report issues, suggest new features, or provide feedback after using the platform.

Mobile Compatibility

A mobile-friendly version of the platform ensuring users can swap shifts on-the-go.

Analytics Dashboard

Insights and analytics for managers to track the frequency of shift swaps and identify any trends or compliance issues.

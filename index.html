ShiftSwap: Shift swaps done fairly

Value Proposition

ShiftSwap enables hourly workers to quickly and fairly exchange shifts with the assurance of manager approval and adherence to company policies, improving workforce flexibility and employee satisfaction.

Features

Shift Exchange Board

A digital bulletin board where employees can post available shifts and browse offers from others.

Manager Approval Workflow

A built-in approval system that notifies managers of proposed shift swaps, ensuring compliance with company policies.

Policy Compliance Check

Automated system that checks all proposed swaps against company policy rules to ensure compliance.

Notification System

Real-time alerts and notifications for employees and managers about shift swap requests and approvals.

Feedback Mechanism

A feature that allows users to provide feedback on their shift swapping experience to improve the platform over time.

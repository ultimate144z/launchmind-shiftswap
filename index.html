```html ShiftSwap: Shift Swaps Done Fairly

ShiftSwap: A user-friendly platform designed to enable hourly workers to quickly and fairly exchange shifts.

Features

Shift Exchange

Enable employees to post their shifts for swapping and browse available shifts from others.

Manager Approval Workflow

Facilitate a structured approval process for shift swaps, enabling managers to review and approve or deny requests easily.

Policy Compliance Checker

Automatically verify that proposed shift swaps comply with company policies and labor regulations before submission.

Notification System

Provide real-time notifications to employees about the status of their swap requests, approvals, and upcoming shifts.

Search and Filter Options

Allow users to search for shifts to swap based on specific criteria such as date, time, and employees available.

User Profiles

Create personalized profiles for employees that include swap history, availability, and preferences.

Feedback Mechanism

Provide a way for users to give feedback on the platform and its features for continuous improvement.
```

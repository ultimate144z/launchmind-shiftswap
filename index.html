ShiftSwap: Shift Swaps Done Fairly

Our Value Proposition

ShiftSwap offers hourly workers an efficient way to exchange shifts with minimal hassle, ensuring manager approval and adherence to workplace policies, thus promoting flexibility and job satisfaction for employees while maintaining operational integrity for employers.

Features

  • Shift Posting: Allows workers to post available shifts for swapping, specifying criteria and availability for potential swappers.
  • Approval Workflow: Facilitates manager approval for each shift swap request, ensuring that all shifts are covered per company policy.
  • Policy Compliance Checker: Automatically checks compliance with company policy regarding shift swaps, notifying users of any issues before submission.
  • Shift Matchmaking: Suggests potential shift swaps based on user availability and preferences to streamline the process.
  • User Ratings and Feedback: Allows workers to rate their swap partners after a shift, fostering a community of trust and accountability.
  • Push Notifications: Sends real-time notifications for new shift postings, approvals, and changes, ensuring users stay informed.
  • Dashboard Analytics: Provides managers with insights into shift swaps and trends, helping to optimize scheduling and workforce management.

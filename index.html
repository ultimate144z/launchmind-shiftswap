ShiftSwap: Shift Swaps Done Fairly

Value Proposition

ShiftSwap provides hourly workers with a convenient and fair platform to easily exchange shifts with approval from management, ensuring compliance with workplace policies and enhancing workforce flexibility.

Features

Shift Exchange Platform

A user-friendly interface where hourly workers can post available shifts and request shifts they need, facilitating quick and equitable exchanges.

Manager Approval System

An integrated approval mechanism allowing managers to review and approve shift swaps to ensure compliance with staffing needs and policies.

Policy Compliance Tracking

Automated tracking features that ensure all shift swaps adhere to company policies while notifying workers and managers of any violations.

Notification System

Real-time notifications for workers and managers when shifts are exchanged, requested, or need approval.

User Rating System

A feedback system where users can rate each other after completing a shift swap to promote accountability and reliability.

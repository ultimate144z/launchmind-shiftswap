ShiftSwap: Empowering Hourly Workers to Exchange Shifts Seamlessly

Shift swaps done fairly.

Value Proposition

ShiftSwap is a flexible and efficient platform that empowers hourly workers to exchange shifts seamlessly while ensuring compliance with company policies and receiving necessary managerial approval.

Features

Shift Posting

Allow hourly workers to post available shifts they wish to swap, including details like time, date, and role required.

Approval Workflow

Facilitate an easy approval process where managers can review and approve requested shift swaps with clear visibility into the implications on staffing.

Notification System

Provide real-time notifications to workers and managers regarding shift swap requests, approvals, denials, and changes in scheduling.

Shift Compliance Checker

Integrate a compliance check that alerts users about potential policy violations during a shift exchange, ensuring adherence to labor laws.

User Profile Management

Enable users to manage their profiles, displaying shift preferences, availability, and past exchanges.

Analytics Dashboard

Provide managers and HR with insights on shift swapping patterns, approval rates, and compliance to help improve workforce management.

ShiftSwap: Seamless Shift Exchange Platform

Shift swaps done fairly.

Our Value Proposition

ShiftSwap provides hourly workers with a seamless and efficient platform to exchange shifts quickly and fairly, ensuring manager oversight and compliance with labor policies, ultimately improving workforce satisfaction and operational efficiency.

Features

Shift Matching Algorithm: Automatically suggests potential shift swaps based on worker availability and skill set, making matching efficient.
Manager Approval Workflow: Enables managers to review and approve requested shift swaps, ensuring compliance and staffing needs are met.
Real-Time Notifications: Sends immediate alerts to workers and managers about shift swap requests, approvals, or changes to ensure everyone is informed.
Compliance Checker: Automatically checks swaps against company policies and labor laws to ensure that all swaps are legal and compliant.
User Profiles: Allows workers to create profiles that showcase their skills, availability, and preferences, enhancing swap accuracy.
Shift History Tracking: Allows users to see past shift swaps and requests, aiding transparency and management review.
Feedback System: Allows users to provide feedback on the swapping process and quality of swaps, facilitating continuous improvement.
